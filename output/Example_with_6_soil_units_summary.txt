4. WARNINGS
==============================================================================
* LOW FITTING: the fitting (R2) read from legend.csv is below 0.90 in 6 of 6 domains (values 0.41-0.49). The fitting divides the variance of the predicted values in the spatial variance, so a low fitting inflates it. Treat any spatial gain with caution.

* SPATIAL VERSION KEPT DESPITE LOW FITTING: in 6 of 6 domains Phase 6b kept the spatial allocation (reduction 395.7 units). With a range of 80-109 km on 1 km cells the prediction errors of the cells of a stratum are treated as strongly correlated and the prediction-variance term of the stratum variance nearly cancels: the reduction comes from the range, not from the fitting. Rely on it only if the range is supported by local data (see the kriging report).

* SPATIAL PARAMETERS NOT VALIDATED: legend.csv classifies 6 of 6 domains as NOT RELIABLE (BA_Cropland, BE_Cropland, DO_Cropland, 60.4_Cropland, 60.4_Forest, 60.7_Cropland). The kriging report recommends spatial_component = FALSE when any soil unit is NOT RELIABLE.
