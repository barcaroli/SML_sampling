==============================================================================
4. WARNINGS
==============================================================================
* LOW FITTING: the variogram fitting (R2) is below 0.90 in 4 of 4 zone x variable fits (range 0.41-0.49). The fitting enters the spatial variance at the denominator, so with a low fitting the spatial component is expected to INCREASE the variance, i.e. to give a sample size not smaller than the non-spatial one. Treat any spatial gain with caution.

* SPATIAL VERSION SELECTED DESPITE LOW FITTING: in 6 of 6 domains Phase 6b kept the spatial allocation (gain 373.0 units). This is not the expected behaviour for fitting < 0.90; check how fitting and range enter the spatial variance (the gain may come from the large range, ~80-109 km on 1 km cells, rather than from the fitting) before relying on it.

* NATIONAL MODEL USED AT ZONE LEVEL: for 4 of 4 zone x variable fits the zone model could not be estimated (fewer than 30 valid survey points, or degenerate fit) and the national model was used. Zones affected: Emilia orientale - delta del Po; Toscana meridionale. The national spatial structure is not confirmed by local data.
