==============================================================================
4. WARNINGS
==============================================================================
* LOW FITTING: fitting below 0.90 in 12 of 12 soil unit x variable combinations (values 0.41-0.49). In the within-stratum variance of SamplingStrata the fitting divides the term of the predicted values, (z_i - z_j)^2 / fitting; a low fitting inflates that term, i.e. it says that the frame values explain little of the true variability.

* LARGE RANGE: in 11 of 12 soil unit x variable combinations the range (80-109 km) is large compared with the mean distance inside the soil unit (11-72 km), so rho > 0.50. In the within-stratum variance the prediction-error term is (s_i^2 + s_j^2 - 2 s_i s_j exp(-kappa d_ij / range)): when the range is much larger than the distances inside a stratum, the errors are treated as almost perfectly correlated and this term nearly cancels. The sample size then decreases because of the range, independently of the fitting: a large range not supported by local data gives a reduction that is not warranted.

* NATIONAL MODEL USED AT ZONE LEVEL: for 4 of 4 zone x variable fits the zone model could not be estimated (fewer than 30 valid survey points, or degenerate fit) and the national model was used. Zones affected: Emilia orientale - delta del Po; Toscana meridionale. The national spatial structure is not confirmed by local data.

==============================================================================
6. NOTES
==============================================================================
* Each soil unit takes the variogram of its zone: soil units of the same zone share fitting and range.
* The fitting written to legend.csv is the R2 of the variogram fit. In SamplingStrata the fitting
  divides the variance of the predicted values, i.e. it plays the role of the R2 of the model that
  produced the frame values; the trend R2 is reported in section 1 for comparison.
* legend.csv can be edited by hand before running the sampling script; only the columns
  zona, soil_unit, fitting_<var> and range_<var>_km are read (range in km).
